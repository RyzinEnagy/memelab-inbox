## Known constraints carried from Phase 00 into Phase 01

- Real dated history is 17 days of sparse thesis snapshots plus collector candles for 33 pools from 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-08 (DATA_COVERAGE.md). Free GeckoTerminal OHLCV reaches back at least to pool creation for recent pools at about 10 calls per minute; DEX Screener has no history.
- Backtesting & Stress-Testing v1.2 is not installed on any branch: BACKTEST_NOT_AVAILABLE (PI-D-006).
- Code reaches the scheduled runs only after a merge into `main` (PI-D-005).
- Stored thesis scores depend on the code version and inputs that produced them, neither of which is recorded (PI-D-008).
- `theses.created_at` is the collector-run start, written a median 16.9 min (max 116.5 min) later; never a decision or availability time without a conservative delay or a recorded capture time (PI-D-011). Launch-cohort outcomes: 19 (24 h), 8 (7 d), 2 (30 d) of 1,422.
- Symbol-only catalyst links and alerts are not identity (PI-D-007, PI-D-012).
- The independent skeptic is run by the orchestrator, then folded in by a repair pass (PI-D-013).

## Known constraints carried from Phase 03 into later phases

- Scores measure fitness for the lab's pipeline on a synthetic challenge, not memecoin forecast skill; on the challenge most models were within noise of the base rate (17 holdout positives). The challenge's 14.6 h embargo band against 24 h labels cannot inflate a metric through label overlap (labels independent by construction); purged versus unpurged differences up to about 0.002 Brier are sample-size noise (placebo test), and the band earns or loses nothing (`phase_03/research/SMOKES/_challenge/NOTE.md`).
- No reviewed library refused the S1 future-feature trap by itself; time discipline stays in the lab's own as-of view, enforced through declared availability (pandera registered checks, feast semantics as an oracle) and label-maturity purge (purgedcv). Defaults that leak and must be banned in wrappers: scikit-learn `CalibratedClassifierCV(cv=None)` and row-gap TimeSeriesSplit, LightGBM `lgb.cv` defaults, statsmodels Markov smoothed probabilities and full-sample parameters, venn-abers estimator mode with `shuffle=True`, tsfresh `rolling_direction<0`, ruptures Window and full-series fits, stumpy self-join, feast without `filter_by_created_timestamp`, river's end-of-stream flush (learned by `progressive_val_score`, so no replay-trained model state is kept), sktime `evaluate` with test-period exogenous rows, purgedcv `PurgedKFold` and CPCV for any model used in replay (they train on later rows) and `PurgedGroupKFold` on this data shape (empty folds), MAPIE's Venn-Abers port and `VennAbersCalibrator(cv="prefit")` with a fitted Pipeline, hazardous `recalibrate_survival_function` (moves the wrong way, unclipped).
- REJECT routes: TabPFN default and newer weights (non-commercial, outputs restricted), hazardous 0.2.0 PyPI artifacts (unknown-rights SEER data, contents not in the source tree), OASIS's bundled Twitter pickles, mlfinpy entirely, MiroFish in process, copied or served. - Runtime limits: NannyML, frouros (release), mlfinpy, MiroFish, OASIS and black-it cannot run on the lab's Python 3.13 or numpy 2 stack; evidently, feast, lifelines and hazardous declare pandas below 3.
