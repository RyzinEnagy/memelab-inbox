## Findings

All MINOR. None changes the phase status or the decision.

- **m1 (CX-S15, the decision-level null is optimistic for mint-level warnings, and the NCB control is planted at the wrong unit to show it).** The rule warnings are functions of a mint's persistent attention proxies, so they cluster by mint, and repeated decisions of one mint have overlapping, correlated label windows. The day-stratified permutation exchanges decisions. An outcome-independent warning assigned per mint gives p below 0.05 (either side) in 13.6% of 1,000 salts on T1b against 5.4% for the same warning assigned per decision (T2 8.2% against 6.8%, T1 7.6% against 5.3%; one-sided up to 7.3%). The phase names the issue in prose and adds independence counts, but no test or control measures it: NCB hashes the decision key. Effect: the raw p values (MAD 0.0045, T2 0.066) are weaker than nominal. Fix for later phases: plant the negative control per mint, or permute mint blocks within day.
- **m2 (NCB passes through a padded Holm family).** The bogus VERIFIED catalyst fires only on T1, yet its p is Holm-adjusted as one of three targets with two fictitious p = 1 entries. Unadjusted it would have been flagged. Read honestly, the control is a single pre-registered test that came out at raw p 0.032; it is a fluke (m1 numbers), but the "not flagged" label rests on the padding. A control should be judged on its own pre-set level, or over many salts.
- **m3 (blindness guard narrower than the wording).** The coding probe blocks `training_labels`, the label stores, the sealed folder and prediction files, but not the p05 feature store (74,541 candles, so post-decision prices) or `data/memelab.sqlite` (market snapshots). "No outcome reachable" holds by the code path (seeds carry only socialcat-1 proxies available at the decision, 0 late observations in 925 seeds) and, for the 830 development documents, by equality with Phase 14's documents, not by the guard.
- **m4 (subgroup prose is one-sided).** The records say the MAD subgroup "reverses" or "goes the other way" on the holdout; 1 hit in 14 against a prevalence of 0.119 is indistinguishable from the prevalence (gate INSUFFICIENT_EVENTS). The same holdout table shows ORGANIC on T1b at 4 of 8 against 0.176 (uncorrected one-sided binomial 0.037), a favourable-looking subgroup the prose does not mention, and the development table shows ATTENTION_EXHAUSTION on T2 at 2 of 32 against 0.214 (binomial 0.021), a rule that points the wrong way, also unmentioned. None is a finding; the prose should say all three are noise rather than point to the one that supports the conclusion.
- **m5 (three tests that cannot fail).** Own mutations X2 (case-insensitive outcome-key match), X3 (dropping `label_observed_at`) and X13 (NO_EVENT labels in `labels_by_target`) survive both Phase 15 test files. X13 has no effect on the outputs because no rule states NO_EVENT. Also `test_coding_was_committed_before_the_scoring_code_and_outputs` returns early, passing, on a shallow or exported tree.
- **m6 (the RULES_CANDIDATE branch is unreachable from the recorded scorer, and the sensitivity N is not stated).** `score_scenarios.py` always passes `p_max_t=None`, so a skill verdict would end CORRECTION_NOT_COMPUTED, never SURVIVES. Protocol 6 says max-T is computed when the verdict is a skill verdict. This is stricter, not looser, and the verdicts were not skill verdicts, but the protocol and the code disagree. The like-for-like Phase 12 sensitivity N (160) is not reported.
- **m7 (registry and ledger do not match in either direction).** As in the G18 judgement above: six non-configuration registry rows have no ledger row (flagged by the phase), and six POST_OPENING ledger rows have no registry row (not flagged).

## Counterexample CX-S15

Script: `/home/claude/pi_orchestration/skeptic/p15_cx_mint_clustered_null.py`; output: `p15_cx_mint_clustered_null_out.json` (1,000 salts, 500 permutation replicates each, the phase's own frozen `scenario_eval.warning_metrics`). A plausible wrong reading: "the NCB control proves the day-stratified permutation is calibrated, so a raw p of 0.0045 for MANUFACTURED_ATTENTION_DUMP is strong development evidence." Planting the same outcome-independent bogus warning per mint instead of per decision shows false-positive rates up to 2.5 times the decision-level rate (T1b 13.6% against 5.4%, both sides at 0.05). Nothing in the phase catches this. The NCB control is planted per decision, the tests use synthetic independent rows, and the independence counts are descriptive. It does not change any Phase 15 verdict, because every E2 verdict is already DESCRIPTIVE_NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE and nothing descriptive reaches `decide`. Any later phase that reads rule-warning p values must use a mint-level null or a mint-planted control.
