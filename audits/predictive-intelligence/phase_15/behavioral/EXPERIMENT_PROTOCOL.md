Written 2026-10-27 from 00:50Z by the Phase 15 agent. Committed with `../ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md`, `../experiments/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.csv`, PI-D-078 (the orchestrator's entry decision) and PI-D-079 (these rules), before any Phase 15 code exists, before any scenario is coded and before any label is read by Phase 15. The contract (`memelab.predictive.forecast/1`, ABSTAIN_REASONS), ACCEPTANCE and the registry fingerprint `4b4aabce...` are not changed.

## 1. Frozen question

Can behavioral simulation (MiroFish, comparator D) generate identifiable, early, source-grounded scenario warnings or features that improve the forecast of the same future outcome beyond approaches without simulation: (A) predeclared rule-based narratives, (B) an evidence-only LLM prompt without simulation, (C) a calibrated statistical classifier, and abstention? The outcomes are the three registered primary targets of `labels-1` (T1 `price_path_target_before_invalidation_24h`, T1b `price_path_target_before_invalidation_6h`, T2 `price_path_severe_drawdown_or_halt_24h`), at their registry horizons (24 h, 6 h, 24 h), with the window starting at the decision. The event population the package requires (MIROFISH_EVALUATION.md) is at least 100 eligible, independently dated catalyst events.

## 2. Eligible catalyst events and the count

An eligible catalyst event is: a catalyst item or event for an exact chain and mint (never a ticker or name match, PI-D-007, PI-D-012); with a first-availability proof (the lab's first sighting `first_seen_at` or the revision log, never a crawl date, P04) at or before a development decision of that mint; with a Phase 10 `classify_catalyst` state as of that decision (VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED_EVENT or IDENTITY_UNVERIFIED; NOT_AVAILABLE_AT_DECISION is not an event); and with a matured, uncensored `labels-1` label for at least one primary target. Independence: items about one mint within 24 h of each other are one event; duplicated items across sources count once (P15).

Count sources, all read without a label: the tracked `data/memelab.sqlite` (every table whose name holds `catal` or `social`), the top-level keys of every export commit that is a development decision (`data/state/latest.json` at that commit), `data/private/` (the private stores), the CATALYST_EVENT observations of the 830 Phase 14 development seeds, and the inbox catalyst reads Phase 10 cross-checked (`phase_10/evidence/crosschecks.json`). Expected, stated in advance: 0 eligible events (Phase 10: no catalyst event, link or assessment exists for any decision). The count and every candidate item that is not eligible, with its reason, go into `EVENT_SET.csv`.

`EVENT_SET.csv` also lists the decision snapshots the non-catalyst comparison uses (one row per development and holdout-window decision with a seed): decision key, mint, decision time, export commit, seed sha256, availability proof (the export commit, PI-D-015), catalyst state, eligible-catalyst flag and reason. These are not catalyst events and are not independent: repeated mints and batched export days are counted (rows, distinct mints, distinct days, distinct export commits).

## 3. Scenario categories

The behavioral-1 label vocabulary, unchanged: CATALYST_FOLLOW_THROUGH, RUMOR_FADE, ATTENTION_EXHAUSTION, ORGANIC_ATTENTION_CONTINUATION, MANUFACTURED_ATTENTION_DUMP, NO_GROUNDED_SCENARIO. Comparator A is `behavioral.rule_scenarios` (behavioral-rules-1, rule table sha256 `557ef8780869b939b21185370c0baf484fdd9603da42618f75d981512828fc19`, R1 to R6 of the Phase 14 checklist rule 15). ## 4. Coding rules (scenario-coding-1, mechanical, blind)

1. Only a document that passes `behavioral.validate_scenario` with status RULES_RAN or SIMULATION_RAN and kind BEHAVIORAL_HYPOTHESIS_NOT_A_FORECAST is coded; any other is NOT_CODABLE with its reason (invalid document, status not ran, free text without the vocabulary such as MiroFish's script output). NOT_CODABLE documents are reported and never counted as a hit or a miss.
2. A hypothesis whose monitor metric is a primary target becomes one statement (decision, target, expectation EVENT or NO_EVENT, label, hypothesis id). Its window must be exactly the decision time to the decision time plus the target's registry horizon; any other window is REFUSED_WINDOW (reported, not scored).
3. A hypothesis whose monitor metric is not a primary target (CATALYST_VERIFIED_BY_WINDOW_END, ATTENTION_TREND_AT_NEXT_DECISION) is UNSCORABLE_NO_OUTCOME_SOURCE in Phase 15 (no catalyst row exists and the attention-trend metric has no frozen outcome definition here): reported, excluded from every numerator and denominator.
4. NO_GROUNDED_SCENARIO (no monitor) is NO_STATEMENT.
5. Warning per decision and target: EVENT when at least one statement expects EVENT and none expects NO_EVENT; NO_EVENT when only NO_EVENT statements exist; CONFLICT when both exist (ambiguous: excluded from numerator and denominator, reported); NONE when there is no statement.
6. Blindness by construction: the coder is code, not a person; it reads only scenario documents and refuses any input holding an outcome-like key (`y`, `outcome...`, `label_class`, `label_value`, `realized...`, `realised...`, `hit`, `first_touch...`, `label_matures_at`, `label_observed_at`, `event_observed`); coding runs in a process where `labels.training_labels` raises, opening `phase_06/sealed/` raises and every socket call raises; the coded output (`SCENARIO_CODING.csv`, `CONTROL_CODING.csv`, `EVENT_SET.csv`, `CODING_LOG.json`) is committed with its sha256 before the scoring step runs, and the scoring step refuses coding files whose sha256 differs from the committed log. This blinds the coding, not the rule author (see 7).

## 6. Evaluation sets (development only, holdout only as POST_OPENING description)

- E1, primary and as-of: the development walk-forward out-of-sample rows of `phase_10/experiments/OOS_PREDICTIONS.csv` (the Phase 07 folds: T1 24 rows, T1b 44, T2 8). Each row's `y` must equal the label `labels.training_labels(dev, holdout_start, target)` gives for the decision (any mismatch stops the run). A_WARN is scored with the frozen `baselines.evaluate` and `verdict` against B0 (Brier skill, day and mint bootstrap, 2000 replicates, seed 20261025, the frozen minimums: 5 days, 50 events, 10 per class) and, on the same rows, against B2; B2, R2 and S2 against B0 are restated from the same rows. Holm over the three primary targets (an undefined test counts as p 1); selection status by `evaluation.selection_status` with the uncorrected verdict, Holm, and the full-search Bonferroni at the new N_search (max-T is not computed: CORRECTION_NOT_COMPUTED unless the verdict is a skill verdict). Expected: INSUFFICIENT_DAYS or INSUFFICIENT_EVENTS on every target.
- E2, descriptive: every development label `labels.training_labels(dev, holdout_start, target)` gives (T1 136, T1b 224, T2 131 expected) joined to the coded warnings. Per target with statements: labelled rows, ambiguous rows excluded, warned rows, hits, precision with Wilson interval, prevalence with Wilson interval, recall, coverage, lift (precision minus prevalence), lead time of hits (`first_touch_start` minus decision time; median, minimum, maximum, in hours), and a day-stratified permutation test (warnings permuted among the decisions of the same UTC decision day, 2000 replicates, seed 20261025; p up = share of permuted lifts at or above the observed, p down = share at or below, each with the +1 rule), Holm over the three targets for each direction. Gates: fewer than 50 labelled, 10 per class or 5 days: INSUFFICIENT_EVENTS or INSUFFICIENT_DAYS; fewer than 20 warned: INSUFFICIENT_WARNINGS; none warned: NO_WARNINGS. Otherwise DESCRIPTIVE_LIFT_SUGGESTED_NOT_A_CLAIM (lift above 0 and Holm p up below 0.05), DESCRIPTIVE_WORSE_THAN_PREVALENCE (lift below 0 and Holm p down below 0.05) or DESCRIPTIVE_NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE. E2 is never a claim and never promotes anything: the rules were not fitted on labels, but the rule table was written in Phase 14 after development and holdout summaries of the same socialcat-1 inputs were public (Phase 10 to 13), so E2 is not out of sample (RULES_AUTHORED_AFTER_DATA_PUBLIC). Per-label rows (each label alone) are secondary descriptions without a verdict beyond the gates.
- E3, POST_OPENING description: the decisions of the opened retrospective holdout (2026-10-17 to 2026-10-24) with a seed built exactly as Phase 14 builds development seeds, coded blind in the coding step; scored in the scoring step against the public `phase_12/validation/HOLDOUT_SCORED.csv` (spent, PI-D-065): warning metrics as in E2 (no lead time: the file has no touch time) and A_WARN fitted at the holdout start on all development labels, Brier against the file's B0 and B2 columns. Logged in `behavioral/POST_OPENING_LOG.json`, combined only through `evaluation.conservative_status`; never a selection, calibration or claim.

## 7. Measures for every comparator

Precision, warning coverage, recall, lead time, hindsight contamination (seeds refused, post-decision content, contamination flags, P26), variance (twin run byte-equality; seed and persona grid for engines that have them), hallucinations (statements citing an id that is not a seed observation, ungrounded catalysts, refused claims), cost and latency (USD, wall time), incremental out-of-sample predictive value (E1), and net decision utility. Net decision utility is NOT_COMPUTABLE here: no historical executable depth exists (NO_HISTORICAL_EXECUTABLE_DEPTH, EXECUTION_DATA_MISSING) and the backtesting engine is BACKTEST_NOT_AVAILABLE for this engine. For B and D every measure is NOT_RUN with the blocking reason.

## 8. Controls

- NCB, bogus catalyst (P45, the package's intentionally bogus catalyst): a fictional CATALYST_EVENT observation (name `bogus_control_catalyst`, value BOGUS_CONTROL_NOT_A_REAL_EVENT, source ref `fictional-bogus-control:<decision key prefix>`, available at the decision) planted in the development seeds whose `sha256(decision_key + ":bogus-control")` first hex digit is even (a choice independent of outcomes). Variant VERIFIED fires R1 (CATALYST_FOLLOW_THROUGH, T1 EVENT); scored like E2 on T1 it must come out DESCRIPTIVE_NOT_DISTINGUISHABLE or below a gate; anything else marks the scoring unreliable (NCB_FAILED). Variant UNVERIFIED_EVENT fires R2 (RUMOR_FADE): every such statement must be UNSCORABLE_NO_OUTCOME_SOURCE, none scored. The lab's real seeds hold no catalyst and the Phase 10 classifier never gives VERIFIED to an unsourced item (its tests); the control tests the evaluation, not the classifier.
- NCS, shuffled seeds: the E2 day-stratified permutation is the null; in E1, A_WARN with warnings permuted within day (one fixed permutation, seed 20261025) is scored and reported as a control.
- P28, leaked future content: on all 830 development seeds, (a) one observation made available one second after the decision and (b) one QUOTED_PUBLIC_TEXT observation holding the date of the T2 window end: each seed must be refused by `check_seed` (expected 830 of 830 for each) and give no statement.
- P26, LLM pretraining: rules NOT_APPLICABLE (no model). For B and D: the evaluated decisions are dated 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-23 and the evaluation would run after them; the lab cannot verify a hosted model's training cutoff or that it has no later retrieval or tuning, and Phase 14 rule 13 flags every such document LLM_PRETRAINING_CONTAMINATION_RISK and EXPLORATORY_NOT_AS_OF. A retrospective MiroFish or LLM evaluation on these dates is therefore not a valid as-of test, whatever its score; only prospective events after a run is committed could be.
- P27, P29, P30: no agreement number exists in any A document (Phase 14 validators); rules are DETERMINISTIC_RULES (the twin run must be byte-equal); B and D end SIMULATION_NOT_AVAILABLE or NOT_SUPPORTED and the core is unaffected (no forecast document changes in Phase 15).

## 10. Decision rule (frozen, `scenario_eval.decide`)

- Validation status of the simulation question: INSUFFICIENT_VALIDATION when the eligible catalyst events are fewer than 100, or D did not run on the event set, or B did not run; otherwise EVALUABLE.
- MiroFish adoption: MIROFISH_GO_CANDIDATE_PROSPECTIVE_ONLY only when the status is EVALUABLE and D's E1-type out-of-sample verdict is a skill verdict that survives the selection correction against B0 and is not worse than A, B, C and abstention, D is STABLE under the frozen grid, every evaluated event is after D's verified training cutoff, and the spend stayed inside an approved budget; otherwise MIROFISH_NO_GO (the module stays OFF and research-optional; the Phase 14 boundary and the research record are kept).
- Rules-1: RULES_CANDIDATE_PROSPECTIVE_ONLY only when A_WARN has an E1 skill verdict that survives the selection correction against B0 and is not worse than B2; otherwise RULES_NOT_PROMOTED (a fallback descriptor, never a model input or a probability). E2 and E3 can never promote anything.
- No decision creates a probability, a score input or a live capability.
