## G. Candidates not shortlisted

Shortlist rule, written down in repair pass 2 from the reasons the first version applied (the reasons themselves did not change; `probes/check_candidates.py` now enforces 2, 4 and 6): (1) the candidate fills a frozen need, and per need and role one or two rows are shortlisted, the most direct implementation first (DUPLICATE_ROLE otherwise); (2) a library that would run in-process must carry a permissive license (MIT, BSD, Apache, ISC, NCSA); copyleft, source-available or non-commercial code terms exclude it, while behavioral simulators, which run only behind the isolated PLANNED_SEAMS adapter, may be copyleft and are flagged; (3) a heavy dependency (JVM, torch as a base requirement, a server stack) excludes a row only when another shortlisted or eligible row covers the role without it; (4) last commit within 18 months and not archived; (5) the need must be live: needs whose targets are BLOCKED_NO_DATA or DEFERRED (N11, N12, N14) are not shortlisted, and model rows must output event probabilities, not value forecasts or anomaly scores; (6) at least 3 rows per high-priority family (tabular, time series, calibration, online and drift, behavioral, experiment audit, event-time labels), and MiroFish and OASIS always (package-mandated); (7) very young projects (about 15 commits or fewer, or 5 files or fewer) are not shortlisted when an established row covers the role (YOUNG_SMALL).
