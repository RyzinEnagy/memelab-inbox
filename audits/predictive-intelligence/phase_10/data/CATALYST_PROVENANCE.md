# Catalyst and social provenance (Phase 10)

What social, narrative and catalyst evidence the lab had at each of its decisions, where it lives, which clock gives its availability, what rights and access rules apply, and what Phase 10 does with it. Status PROVISIONAL under PI-D-046. Written from the code and data at `c3f04c8` (read only: `memelab/social/`, `memelab/catalyst/`, `memelab/chains/narrative.py`, `memelab/modules/m19_attention_analysis.py`, `m20_manipulation_detection.py`, `m21_lifecycle_classifier.py`, `m24_opportunity_ranker.py`, `docs/social-intelligence/SOURCE_COVERAGE.md`, `docs/catalyst/FIRECRAWL.md`) and the real history of `origin/main` exports. Real row counts come from `evidence/crosschecks.json` and `experiments/NARRATIVE_COVERAGE_BY_DAY.csv` of the recorded run.

## The answer first

- No social item, observation, edit, deletion, token resolution, claim, trader link, wallet attribution or trader score exists for any of the lab's 1,500 decisions in anything this initiative can read. Every social table is outside the state export (Phase 00 DATA_COVERAGE), `data/private/` (the signal ledger and private social store) does not exist in this checkout, and every social route is BUILT on fixtures only or BLOCKED on credentials and approvals (SOURCE_COVERAGE.md). FACT.
- No catalyst event, item, revision, token link, attention sample, assessment or outcome exists for the decisions either: the catalyst tables are not exported and each scheduled catalyst pass starts empty (FIRECRAWL.md "Gaps and open items"). FACT.
- What the lab did record at each decision, inside the exported thesis and token rows, is an attention reading built from on-chain and market proxies (holder growth, unique traders, Jupiter organic score, a paid DEX Screener boost), a manipulation screen, a lifecycle stage, the token's provider-reported channels and a name-keyword narrative tag, plus the narrative tables of the same export. Phase 10 uses only these, as `socialcat-1`, available at the export commit that is the decision (PI-D-015). They are proxies of attention, not social text. FACT for the record, HEURISTIC for what the proxies mean.
- So every real decision carries `catalyst_state` CATALYST_TABLES_NOT_EXPORTED and `social_state` SOCIAL_TABLES_NOT_EXPORTED. No catalyst is VERIFIED on any decision, no catalyst probability exists, and the catalyst classifier and the social evidence adapter are TEST_ONLY.
- No page was crawled, no Firecrawl credit spent, no API called, no language model used. A crawl made today could not stand in for what existed at a past decision anyway (DATA_TIME_CONTRACT, P04).

## Reused social-intel seams (PI-D-017), unchanged

- `replay_core.Audit` and `LeakageError`: every row the decision path reads goes through `features.AsOfView` (which wraps them), and every catalyst item, revision, crawled page, token link, social observation and token resolution the adapters read is checked by an `Audit` at T.
- `alpha_core.wilson`: the trader track-record lower bound.
- Not reused, with reasons: the alpha event study's `evaluate` (it scores sources on calls with matched comparison tokens and needs real claims and candles, none exist here); the outcomes engine's `evaluate` (Phase 06 labels already follow its semantics for the frozen targets, PI-D-017); `identity.resolve_*` (the adapter reads stored resolutions; it never resolves anything itself). No social, catalyst, chains or modules file is changed (`git diff f99c6c7 HEAD` on those folders is empty, `evidence/crosschecks.json`).

## Point-in-time rules applied (scenarios)

| Scenario | Rule | Where it is enforced and tested |
|---|---|---|
| P03 edited post | an item's content is its ORIGINAL version recorded by T; an item whose only visible version is an EDIT is excluded | `social_as_of`; `test_p03_*` |
| P04 page crawled today | a crawled page counts only when fetched by T; `published_at` is never availability; narrative tables from another export are refused | `classify_catalyst`, `narrative_values`; `test_p04_*`, `test_cx10_*`, runner trap P04-SC |
| P15 same content across sources | catalyst: only ORIGINAL and INDEPENDENT roles count, deduplicated by url and body hash; social: originals deduplicated by content hash, accounts of one trader are one source | `test_p15_a_duplicated_influencer_article_counts_once`, `test_social_counts_exact_originals_once_and_never_after_t` |
| P16 bot or paid attention | lab readings: paid boost or manufactured flow sets `sc_attention_suspect`, and suspect acceleration counts as no acceleration; social: BOT and SUSPENDED accounts excluded and reported, attention confidence LOW above half | `test_p16_*` |
| P17 fake or deleted rumor | RUMOR evidence, a denial, a cancellation or a deleted source by T gives UNVERIFIED_EVENT; a social item deleted by T is excluded | `test_p17_*` |
| P26 language-model knowledge | no model judgment anywhere; claims extracted by a model are excluded and counted | `test_p26_*`; every card carries `llm_used: false` |
| P36 a value after its availability | every value carries its availability (T); the snapshot `t` after T refuses the row; the allowlist and `validate_rows` refuse a later value | `test_p36_*`, `test_training_rows_*`, runner traps NC2-SC and P36-SC |
| P41 hostile page naming a contract | a mint named only on a crawled page is PAGE_NAMED_ONLY, not identity | `test_p41_*` |
| P45 invented catalyst | an event with no source item seen by T is NOT_AVAILABLE_AT_DECISION; no probability without VERIFIED | `test_p45_*`, `test_verified_needs_*` |
| PI-D-007, PI-D-012 ticker and name links | only NAMED_IN_SOURCE / STRONG with the exact mint in a visible original source is identity; TICKER mentions never count | `test_ticker_or_name_matches_are_never_identity`, `test_ticker_only_mentions_*` |

## What would change this

Forward collection of social items and catalyst events into a store this engine can read, with the clocks above, is the only route to a real catalyst or social evaluation. It needs Elving's approvals (credentials, consent, scheduled jobs; PI-D-005) and weeks of forward data. Until then any catalyst target stays a proposal (`models/CATALYST_TARGET_PROPOSALS.md`).
