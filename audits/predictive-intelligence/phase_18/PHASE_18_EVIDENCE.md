## Limitations

- No real probability exists, so the FORECAST display path, reevaluate and forecast_withdrawn are TEST_ONLY; the examples of a validated forecast are format-only fixtures.
- The request record does not keep the feature availabilities, and rule 14 keeps the calibration module out of the reader, so a report cannot re-run `verify_point`; a FORECAST number is tied to the point stored with the raw number at publication, the hash-chained state and the registry-resolved cards. A writer who rewrites the stored point and the document together and re-seals the chain is not caught (skeptic minor 5, attack A3 still passes): the state is hash-chained, not signed. Since CP18-2 the report says so instead of claiming a recomputation. Carried to Phase 19 or 20: recompute the point from the raw number in a reader that may import the calibration module, or sign the state.
- Token text is withheld when its joined letters contain a trading word, so some harmless symbols (for example one containing "long" or "short" inside another word) are withheld too; the exact mint always identifies the token.
- The replay report has no lab database: its snapshots are not verified and lab facts are UNKNOWN.

- The daily caps were not reached on real data (8 warnings, warning cap 8); the caps, cooldown, dedupe, quiet hours, FEED_CHANGED and CLOCK_BEHIND are proven on fixtures.
