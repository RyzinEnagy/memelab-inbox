## Frozen rules of registry-1 and the champion policy (fixed here, before any code)

1. Candidates. The 69 frozen holdout-start cards of `phase_12/validation/CANDIDATES.json`, each loaded through its family's loader (model sha recomputed) with its file sha256 equal to the recorded one; no refit, no tuning. Valid out-of-sample evidence: the development walk-forward verdicts of Phases 07 to 11 and the Phase 12 first-opening evaluation (primary and secondary rows, with `POST_OPENING_CORRECTIONS.csv` selection statuses). tech-1 cards fitted on T1b have no holdout evaluation (NO_OUT_OF_SAMPLE_EVIDENCE); unfitted cards are NOT_FITTED. No behavioral document, rule warning or simulation is a candidate.
2. Champion per target, no re-selection. The Phase 12 development rule is restated from `CANDIDATES.json` (`champions`: B2 by default, `basis` "default: no candidate earned champion_over_B2 on development data") and checked against `evaluation.champion` on the committed development rows; no holdout outcome is an input. The champion policy then applies: a model is the deployed champion of a target only when its registry level is SHADOW or VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL (rule 3). Otherwise the target's deployed mode is NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION: every request abstains with model `not_selected`, and two references are pinned beside it: B0 (training prevalence, the base-rate reference) and the Phase 12 default (B2, the simple-model reference that any later model must beat). References are scored only into internal records for the prospective comparison (shadow baseline mode, Phase 19) and are never published as a probability or shown as a champion. Expected: NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION for all three targets.
3. Levels, computed by `registry.promotion_decision` from an entry's facts, never given:
   - SHADOW needs all of: leakage, provenance and contract tests pass; training counts at the ACCEPTANCE minimums (100 events, 20 per class); a FIRST_OPENING retrospective holdout evaluation with at least 50 events and 10 per class, calibration in the large and in every bin passed, and BSS point at least 0 (ACCEPTANCE `promotion.SHADOW`); and, because the retrospective holdout cannot support a promotion on its own (PI-D-065 item 3, PI-D-069 item 4), a CALIBRATED calibration card of the same model on admissible prospective evidence (at least 50 events and 10 per class, not the fit set). POST_OPENING facts can only block.
   - VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL needs SHADOW plus: BSS lower 95% bound above 0 after Holm on the retrospective and on the prospective holdout (at least 50 events), Brier point not worse than B2, selection status SURVIVES_SELECTION_CORRECTION over the cumulative search; a model other than B2 replacing B2 also needs the lower bound of its Brier difference to B2 above 0 (`champion_over_B2`).
   - Otherwise: ABSTAIN for the two reference baselines (B0, B2), NO_GO for every other candidate (reason NO_INCREMENTAL_PREDICTIVE_EDGE, plus NOT_FITTED or NO_OUT_OF_SAMPLE_EVIDENCE where they apply). An entry whose stored level differs from the recomputed one is refused.
   - Publishable only with level SHADOW or VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL and a CALIBRATED calibration card; expected publishable false for all 69.
4. Entries. Immutable and hash-addressed: `entry_id` is the sha256 of the canonical entry without `entry_id`, the file is `registry/entries/<entry_id>.json`. Content: registry version, target and target version, target registry fingerprint, family (phase, module version, kind, candidate id), model id, model sha, card path and file sha256, feature set sha and version, family version, training cutoff, last training decision and label maturity, n and k of training, labels version, label set sha, dataset (data snapshot) sha, fitted flag, calibration card (path, card sha, status, level, details) or none, evidence (development verdicts, first-opening retrospective result with source file sha256, prospective status, POST_OPENING references), minimum support (ACCEPTANCE gates), risk flags, role, level with reasons, registry status, publishable flag, inference check (rule 7), retrain and rollback policy reference, `registered_as_of_utc` (a fixed time, no clock). A changed byte refuses the entry (ENTRY_HASH_MISMATCH).
5. Event log. `registry/REGISTRY_LOG.jsonl`, append-only, each event carrying the sha256 of the previous line (genesis `0` x 64) and its own `event_id`; events REGISTER, PIN, ROLLBACK, ENSEMBLE_DECISION. A pin names the target, the mode (CHAMPION or NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION), the champion entry and its inference hash (or none), the reference entries and the reasons. `resolve(target, decision_time)` uses the last PIN or ROLLBACK with `as_of` at or before the decision; a log whose chain breaks, whose earlier lines changed or that names an unknown entry is refused.
6. Compatibility (P46). `registry.compatibility` compares a request and the running code with the entry: feature set sha of the request, feature set sha of the family's current code, feature version, family module version, target registry fingerprint, labels version. A request mismatch is FEATURE_SCHEMA_MISMATCH; a code mismatch (a schema or feature change without retrain) is MODEL_INCOMPATIBLE_RETRAIN_REQUIRED; both refuse use and resolution falls back (rule 9).
7. Identical-inference hash. For every entry, the outputs of its own family `predict` (through `evaluation.score`, so the time checks run) on the probe set: the inputs of the 176 Solana retrospective-holdout decisions per target as Phase 12's `run_holdout.Inputs` builds them (inputs only, no label read). The outputs must equal the committed `phase_12/validation/HOLDOUT_PREDICTIONS.csv` rows exactly (number by `repr`, reason string); the hash is the sha256 of the canonical list of (decision key, number or null, reason). Phase 07 entries also carry the hash on a synthetic probe set committed in `tests/fixtures/predictive/registry_probe_examples.json` (no real data), which the tests recompute. A pin carries the champion's hash; a recomputed hash that differs is INFERENCE_MISMATCH and refuses the pin; a number that is not finite without a reason, or outside [0, 1], is MALFORMED_SCORE (P38).
8. States and thresholds (model level; request-level conditions stay in `abstention-1`):
   - RETROSPECTIVE_MODEL_NOT_AVAILABLE_AS_OF: decision before the training cutoff.
   - MODEL_STALE: decision minus cutoff above ACCEPTANCE `max_model_age_s` (1,209,600 s); for every frozen card, decisions after 2026-10-31T00:00:00Z.
   - DRIFT_ALERT: a Page-Hinkley alarm of Phase 13's drift rule (delta 0.005, threshold 2.0, at least 30 observations, increase only) at or before the decision.
   - MODEL_NOT_PROMOTED: level not SHADOW or VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL.
   - OUT_OF_DISTRIBUTION: per request, any of abstention-1's MISSING_REQUIRED_INPUT, FEATURE_OUT_OF_RANGE (|z| above 4) or SCORE_OUTSIDE_CALIBRATION_RANGE (contract reason OOD); the registry names the thresholds and a test pins them to the abstention and calibration constants.
   - Retrain requirements: MODEL_STALE needs a retrain plan committed before its evaluation data are read; DRIFT_ALERT needs a retrain and a human review; MODEL_INCOMPATIBLE_RETRAIN_REQUIRED needs a retrain before any use; a target or label version change needs a retrain and a new evaluation. Retraining is never automatic (no scheduled job is approved); a retrained card is a new entry that starts at ABSTAIN and inherits no level.
9. Rollback. On DRIFT_ALERT, INFERENCE_MISMATCH, MODEL_INCOMPATIBLE_RETRAIN_REQUIRED or a refused entry, the target falls back to the previous pinned champion whose checks hold, else to NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION with the references; a ROLLBACK event records the reason; earlier events stay byte-equal. Shown on fixtures (a fixture champion rolled back to the fixture baseline and then to abstention) and on a temporary copy of the real log.
10. Publication path. `abstention.forecast_result` gets the card, level and calibration card that the registry resolves; with NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION that is `card=None`, `level="ABSTAIN"`, no calibration card, so the document is ABSTAIN with MODEL_NOT_SELECTED and model `not_selected`. `calibration.py` is not changed: VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL stays refused there, and the change control that lets a VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL calibration card through must make it depend on a registry entry whose recomputed level is VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL (carried to Phase 19).
11. Target selection per opportunity. Eligible outcomes: the PRIMARY targets of the registry fingerprint (T1 24 h, T1b 6 h, T2 24 h) for a Solana decision; DEFERRED and PROPOSED targets are TARGET_NOT_FEASIBLE. Every primary target gets a document with its attempted target and horizon; missing data and the abstain conditions of abstention-1 apply; `backtest_assessment_ref` only when backtesting is VERIFIED (today BACKTEST_NOT_AVAILABLE); `behavioral_scenarios` is a separate list, empty while MIROFISH_NO_GO.
12. Ensemble rule. Members' numbers only from chronologically out-of-fold rows (each member's committed number from its own fold card at the same fold cutoff, the row decided after it); never in-sample; a member that abstains makes the ensemble abstain; members may not share an evidence source (a declared map from every input column to its source; an undeclared column refuses). Registered, per target: ENS_MEAN_B0_B2 and ENS_MEAN_B2_S1 (equal-weight means; S1_SC_LOGIT reads only the lab's attention proxies, disjoint from B2's thesis row), STACK_CHRONO_B2_S1 (logistic on the members' clipped logits, fitted on the out-of-fold rows of earlier folds under the training gate of 100 events, 20 per class and 5 days, evaluated on the next fold). Traps that must be refused: ENS_MEAN_B2_M1_S1 (the lab's fatal flags enter through B2's `thesis_fatal_present` and M1's `oc_structural_fatal` and `oc_distribution_fatal`), ENS_MEAN_B2_M2_S2 (B2's inputs three times), STACK_IN_SAMPLE (stack fitted and scored on the same rows). Development: the frozen `baselines.evaluate` and `verdict` against B0 and against B2 (2,000 replicates, seed 20261025, Holm across the three targets). POST_OPENING: the two means on the committed holdout rows, description only. Decision: ENSEMBLE only when an ensemble earns SKILL_INDICATED_DEVELOPMENT_ONLY against B2, survives the selection correction and could pass rule 3; else NO_ENSEMBLE. Complexity (members, parameters), inference cost and latency (CPU per decision on the probe set, machine dependent, descriptive) and stability (fold cards against the frozen card on the probe set) are reported.
13. Search ledger. Cumulative from `phase_15/experiments/SEARCH_LEDGER.csv` plus `SEARCH_LEDGER_RP2_ADDITION.csv`; Phase 16 adds the 9 ensemble configurations as kind ABLATION (counted candidates), the 9 traps as TRAP_OR_CHECK, the POST_OPENING descriptions as POST_OPENING_CHECK and every debug run. N_search primary 121 becomes 130; sensitivity 160 becomes 169.
14. Today. The registry's answer for every primary target at 2026-10-27T00:00:00Z on the prospective decisions of the development store (inputs missing by design, no label read) and the standing state at 2026-10-31T00:00:01Z (MODEL_STALE for every reference).

## Counterexample CX-16 (named before building)

A plausible wrong registry reads Phase 12's `champion: P07_B2` and writes "champion B2" into the deployed slot of every target, and for T2 shows B2's holdout BSS point of +0.29 as "skill" (its calibration bins passed and the point is positive). A reader would take B2 as a model with skill, and a later phase could publish its number once the flag is on. It is wrong three ways: B2 is the champion by default, not by evidence; T2 has 7 positives against the minimum of 10, so its BSS is INSUFFICIENT_EVENTS whatever the point says; and B2 is UNCALIBRATED (Phase 13). Caught by: `promotion_decision` (count gate and prospective calibration), the champion policy (no level above ABSTAIN means NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION, B2 only as a reference), `verify_entry` (a stored level or a champion pin above the recomputed level is refused) and an output test that reads every pin and every document. A test builds exactly this registry and shows each check refusing it.

## Expected result, stated in advance

No candidate reaches SHADOW: B2 fails the SHADOW criteria on the first-opening retrospective evaluation for every target (BSS point below 0 on T1 and T1b, calibration failed on T1b, counts on T2) and no calibration card is CALIBRATED. Every target is NO_VIABLE_CHAMPION; B0 and B2 are ABSTAIN references; the other 63 entries are NO_GO. The ensemble decision is NO_ENSEMBLE: development out-of-fold rows are INSUFFICIENT_DAYS or INSUFFICIENT_EVENTS, stacking never clears its training gate, and nothing beats the base rate. Under the frozen promotion rule a model trained after 2026-10-17 has no unopened retrospective holdout, so no model can reach SHADOW or VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL without a change control to ACCEPTANCE `promotion` (Elving's or the orchestrator's decision). Phase status: NEEDS_REVIEW until the skeptic. The registry, the policy, the inference check and the ensemble ablation are real and apply to the real cards; the promotion branch (SHADOW, VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL, a pinned champion and its rollback) exists only on fixtures and is recorded as a TEST_ONLY part, as Phase 13 recorded its publication path. PASS after the skeptic only if every gate item holds.
