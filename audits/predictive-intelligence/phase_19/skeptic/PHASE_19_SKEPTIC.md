## Findings

### MATERIAL

**M1. The frozen 48 h revision re-read of the Phase 19 reads is owed (from 2026-10-29T14:35Z) and was taken out of the gate by the phase itself; by the Phase 06 precedent it keeps Phase 19 short of PASS unless the orchestrator or Elving records a decision.**
- PI-D-018 is frozen policy for every label run: "at least 10% of series re-read 48 h or more later to detect revisions". Phase 06 treated the same item as a gate item: PI-D-041 item 5 says "Phase 06 stays short of PASS on this item unless the orchestrator or Elving records another decision"; PI-D-045 item 8 kept Phase 06 NEEDS_REVIEW for it alone; PI-D-046 (Elving's instruction) let Phase 07 start with every result PROVISIONAL and `revision_check_claimed: false`; PI-D-059 ran the frozen probe and only then recorded PASS. Phase 06 also froze the command (`make_labels.py reread48`, refusing to run before 48 h) and the sample file before the deadline.
- Phase 19's pre-registration (checklist rule 6, F5) declares the re-read "owed later and planned"; it is in no gate item (G7, G8, G14 pass without it), PHASE_19_RESULTS.json lists it as a limitation with `blockers: []`, and no PI-D record carries it. There is no frozen 48 h command in `fetch_labels.py` (`cmd_reread` is the same-session check with `min_gap_s=0`), no committed sample file, and the 61 labels and the descriptive numbers carry no provisional marker.
- The science impact is small: every verdict is INSUFFICIENT_DAYS and no label flip can change that; the same-session re-read (0 of 2,996 candles) and my 2-call spot check (0 of 1,999) show no change. Phase 12 also passed on sealed holdout labels that were never in Phase 06's re-read sample, so a carry is defensible. But a phase agent granting itself the exemption that Phase 06 needed an orchestrator or Elving decision for is a quietly moved gate.
- Remedy, either: (a) keep Phase 19 NEEDS_REVIEW until the re-read runs from 2026-10-29T14:35Z; or (b) the orchestrator or Elving records a decision like PI-D-046 that carries it, with a frozen `reread48` command (refusing before 48 h after each first read, sample fixed now: the lowest ceil(10%) of the OK read URLs by sha256, which is the same 3 reads as the same-session sample), Phase 19's outcomes and descriptive numbers marked PROVISIONAL_PENDING_REVISION_REREAD, and the rule that a changed label is a revision entry followed by a rerun of `evaluate`, `drift` and `report` that supersedes the outputs. Survivor K8 below must be fixed first, because the re-read relies on that path.

**M2. Gate item G19 (every mutation caught) does not hold under independent mutation: 10 of 14 survive, 9 of them non-equivalent, in the ledger rules the phase exists to provide.**
- The phase's 46 of 46 reproduces, but my own 14 mutations through the same harness leave K4, K7, K8, K9, K11, K12, K13, K15 and K17 alive (K3 is equivalent while every ledger is loaded through `load`). The most important: K8 (a later read that changes a LABELED class without changing the state is never recorded as a revision; the frozen 48 h re-read and the maturity runs depend on this path), K11 and K15 (identity of a PREDICTION or a reused decision key, P01, not pinned by any test), K4 (`scorable` at an earlier `as_of` would include outcomes recorded later, so a historical evaluation could look ahead).
- Precedent: Phase 06 M1 (14 of 17 survivors on frozen rules) was MATERIAL. Repair is tests only; no recorded output changes.

### MINOR

- **m1 (CX-SK19, see below). Rule 4(e) says a final outcome is revised by "a later read"; `check_payload` only requires `revision_of` and a non-empty reason.** A revision citing the same read with a flipped class (A5), a revision of a LABELED outcome to a censored state with no read (A6), a PENDING superseded before its maturity by a censored state with no read (A7) and a LABELED outcome naming a read that does not exist (A8b) are all accepted, verify, and pass the 37 Phase 19 tests. Only `probes/rebuild_outcomes.py` catches them, and it is neither a test nor part of the post-commit checks. Fix: a revision of LABELED needs a new response key retrieved after the revised outcome's read; a censored or VENDOR_OR_ROUTE_GAP state after maturity needs the failing read's URL and status; run the rebuild (no network, about 20 s) in the outputs test or the post-commit list for every later maturity run.
- **m2. The chain has no automated external anchor.** A consistent rewrite (A3: a prediction changed and the chain re-sealed, manifest rewritten) and a truncate-and-reappend (A4) pass every test; `test_git_order...` checks only that `765eadb`'s ledger has no OUTCOME. Add a test that `git show 765eadb:` of the manifest and ledger equals the current manifest and the current ledger prefix, and that each committed ledger version is `is_append_only` of the previous one. Git history (public, pushed) does anchor it for a human; the packet's claim "cannot be edited or back-filled without detection" should say "without detection by git history or the rebuild".
- **m3. D1 summary and notice (SR-2, disclosed, not fixed).** Every target's notice says "feature monitor OK" while liquidity, market cap, volume and token age were not measurable (28 or 29 values against 30). The notice should say "OK on 1 of 5 columns; 4 INSUFFICIENT_DATA".
- **m4. D6 counts attempts (SR-1, disclosed).** Self-inflicted 429s and the revision re-read count as source failures, so the notices announce SOURCE_FAILURE although every planned read succeeded (0 of 25 final reads failed). Conservative, but not the "non-alarmist details" the prompt asks for. A change control should count final reads and report attempts beside them.
- **m5. `response` for a pinned champion with SUPPORT_BELOW_MINIMUM returns NO_CHANGE**, while checklist rule 10 says SUPPORT_BELOW_MINIMUM gives KEEP_ABSTAIN. Fixture-only path (no champion exists); record the intended reading or align the code.
- **m6. The report's prospective table** prints BSS +0.264 and +0.419 and the mint intervals (T1 0.036 to 0.498, T2 0.117 to 0.761, both above zero) with only the verdict column; the "descriptive only, the mint interval is not the governing interval, no claim" sentence that PI-D-105 and HANDOFF carry is missing from the deliverable a reader is most likely to open.
- **m7. Contact disclosure is narrower than the contact.** `contact_scan.py` reads only Phase 04 to 06 raw manifests. The state exports themselves carry post-decision snapshot prices of repeat mints: 9aqmJj's 2026-10-25 decision row is 23.97 h after its 2026-10-24 entry and was visible to the agent's feasibility build before pre-registration. Adding 9aqmJj to CONTACT_EXCLUDED gives BSS +0.241, +0.006, +0.398 (n 17, 17, 11): immaterial, but the disclosure should name it and the general route (any repeat mint in the window).
