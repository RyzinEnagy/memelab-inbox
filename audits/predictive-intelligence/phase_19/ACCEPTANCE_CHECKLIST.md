## Frozen rules of monitoring-1 (fixed here, before any code)

1. Order. (a) This pre-registration commit. (b) The frozen code commit (module, tests, probes). (c) The predictions commit: every frozen card and the 20 NC1 controls on every prospective Solana decision and every target, the decision attributes, the pre-opening traps, the replay audit, the integration documents and records of the window, the outcome ledger's PLAN, PREDICTION_SET, PREDICTION and DOCUMENT entries, and `monitoring/PREREG_MANIFEST.json` with the sha256 of each. (d) Only then the label reads: the reader refuses to start unless the tree is clean, the manifest equals HEAD's and every file matches its hash; it writes `monitoring/OPENING_LOG.json` (FIRST_OPENING, HEAD, manifest sha, time) before its first call. Any later read of the same window is a REPRODUCTION or a later maturity run, logged. Labels and raw bodies stay in the private store until (c) is committed.
2. Predictions. Each frozen card through its family's own `predict` inside `evaluation.score` (card trained after a decision refused, MODEL_STALE after 14 days), inputs built as in Phase 12 (`run_holdout.py` functions, read-only stores, socket audit hook). No number is chosen, edited or filtered by hand; abstentions (OOD, INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY, NOT_FETCHED, MODEL_STALE) are kept with their reason. The traps of Phase 12 run on the window: NC2 future feature and allowlist, P13 (holdout-start card on a development decision, cutoff moved after a decision, training label after the cutoff), the B2 refit equal to the frozen card. Expected: 0 failures.
3. System documents. The integration-1 export replay (Phase 17 probe, research mode, `unshare -n`) at the frozen code; its documents and records must be byte-equal to the Phase 18 published run (documents `9b839e76...`, records `a8114c19...`); a difference is PUBLICATION_REVISION and stops the phase. Each document enters the ledger as a DOCUMENT entry (forecast id, record id, sha256 of the document line and the record line, status, reasons, target, chain, mint, decision time, `row_sha256` linking it to the decision row).
4. The outcome ledger `outcome-ledger-1` (`monitoring/OUTCOME_LEDGER.jsonl`, rendered in `OUTCOME_LEDGER.md`): one JSON line per entry, sequence number, previous line sha256 and its own sha256 (a chain from genesis); kinds PLAN, PREDICTION_SET, PREDICTION, DOCUMENT, OUTCOME, MONITOR, RESPONSE. Rules: (a) append only; a changed, removed or reordered line is LEDGER_TAMPERED; (b) a PREDICTION or DOCUMENT id appears once; the same id with other content is PREDICTION_EDIT_REFUSED, the identical content is a no-op; (c) a PREDICTION or DOCUMENT for a (decision, target) that already has an OUTCOME is LATE_PREDICTION_REFUSED, whatever time it claims (order is the sequence, not a timestamp); (d) an OUTCOME needs the decision in the ledger's PREDICTION_SET; a LABELED outcome needs a label 0 or 1, `label_observed_at` and the entry's record time at or after `label_matures_at` (P05), the label read's URL, response key and body sha256; a PENDING or censored outcome carries no label (P37); (e) a final outcome (LABELED, censored, ineligible) is never replaced: a later read that changes it is a new OUTCOME with `revision_of` and a reason, both kept, the newest used and flagged REVISED; a PENDING outcome is superseded by the next outcome of the same pair (`supersedes`); (f) identity is chain plus exact mint (P01): an OUTCOME whose mint differs from the PREDICTION's for the same decision key is refused.
5. Outcomes. One OUTCOME per prospective decision and target with a state: LABELED, PENDING (not matured at the read, or read before maturity), NOT_READ, THINNED, MARKET_INACTIVE, VENDOR_OR_ROUTE_GAP or UNVERIFIABLE_NO_VENDOR_HISTORY (censored), NO_SNAPSHOT_PRICE, OUT_OF_SCOPE_CHAIN. ## Counterexample CX-19 (new, wrong but plausible)

A "backdated prediction": after the labels are read, someone appends a PREDICTION for a decision whose outcome is already in the ledger, giving it a `recorded_at` from before the label read, so that a timestamp-based check would accept it as made in advance. Caught by rule 4(c) (order is the ledger sequence: any PREDICTION or DOCUMENT after an OUTCOME of the same pair is refused) and by the git order (the PREDICTION_SET's commit precedes the first logged label call; `probes/run_prospective.py audit` checks it). A test builds the backdated entry and asserts LATE_PREDICTION_REFUSED.
