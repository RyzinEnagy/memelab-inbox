Findings:

1. Route matters. 39 of 274 read CURRENT_VENDOR_LIST decisions are gaps against 2 of 521 PIT_RECORDED ones. In 28 of the 38 identity-OK gaps the vendor's current top pool had its last candle 11 to 14 days before the decision (7 more: about 2 h before, outside the reference hour) and the thesis snapshot carried no 24 h volume to call the market quiet: today's top pool is not the pool that traded then (for example a later migration), so the decision is censored, never labelled from the wrong pool. This is the survivorship risk the registry named; every result is stratified by route, and the CURRENT_VENDOR_LIST labelled share differs (T1: 3 positives of 34 against 31 of 102).
2. The universe is the lab's own thesis decisions, already filtered by its scanner (stage-1 thresholds, watchlist re-analyses), not all Solana tokens. Labels describe outcomes after the lab looked, not the market.
3. Thinning removes most decisions (T1: 568 of 704 eligible development decisions): the scheduled runs re-analyse the same mints many times a day; one label per mint per window is kept (P20, P35).
4. No identity failure: every OHLCV request carried `token=<mint>` and every 200 development body passed the identity check as OK_TOKEN_PARAM with the mint as `meta.base` (0 refused; the stricter orientation rule of repair pass 2 refuses none). P0_FAR_FROM_SNAPSHOT_PRICE: 0 development rows.
5. Vendor retention: the reads reach back to 2026-09-26 (earliest candle served); how long GeckoTerminal keeps 5-minute candles stays UNKNOWN.

## Assumptions and limits

- Provider-lag reconstruction (300 s, HEURISTIC) decides which candle was available at the decision, as in Phase 05.
- A response is assumed contiguous newest-first between its earliest candle and its boundary (the basis of absence evidence); the skeptic's Phase 05 probes support the far end being time-bounded, not gaps inside a page.
- Highs and lows of a response's earliest candle depend on the request window; no label used such a candle (WINDOW_EDGE_CANDLE 0).
- `label_observed_at` is a 2026-10-24 retrieval time; it is never a claim that the label was available at maturity (P04, P07). Training in provider mode uses `label_matures_at`; capture mode would need forward reads.
- No catalyst, holder or depth data: the "delayed confirmation" trace is a price shape, and no catalyst is verified (BLOCKED_NO_DATA).
