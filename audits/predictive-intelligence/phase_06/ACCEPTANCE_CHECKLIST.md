## Rules settled before any label (change control CC-03, recorded as PI-D-040)

1. Absence needs evidence (PI-D-039 applied to the frozen classes). NO_TRADES_AFTER_ENTRY (T1, T1b) and TRADING_HALT (T2) are absences of candles. An absence over a span counts only on an identity-checked read whose boundary is at or after the end of the span (rounded up to the candle grid) and that returned a candle starting before the span (the vendor answers newest first, so every candle between that one and the boundary was served). Otherwise the label is UNVERIFIABLE_NO_VENDOR_HISTORY (censored, conservative lens). An empty or short page alone never makes a halt or a no-trade label.
2. T2 other pools: "up to 3 other vendor-listed pools" are the first three pools, other than the reference pool, of the vendor's current pool list for the mint (`/networks/solana/tokens/<mint>/pools`, page 1, vendor order), read with `token=<mint>` over the halt span. A candle on any of them in the span makes the decision NO_SEVERE_EVENT; each of them needs the absence evidence of rule 1, else the halt is UNVERIFIABLE_NO_VENDOR_HISTORY. They are read only when the reference pool shows an evidenced halt and no drawdown; a halt that comes with a drawdown is kept as a reference-pool flag only.
3. A candle is in the halt span [t_entry + 18 h, t_entry + 48 h) when it overlaps it (start before the span end and end after the span start).
4. One read per decision: eligibility, reference price and every target of one decision come from one response (reads may be shared by decisions of the same pool and archive), so targets of one decision never mix reads whose window-dependent opens, highs or lows can differ (PI-D-037). Cross-target invariants are checked (below).
5. Split per target: DEVELOPMENT (decision before 2026-10-17T00:00Z and `label_matures_at` at or before it), PURGED (decision before it, maturity after: no label is computed), HOLDOUT (decision in the window), PROSPECTIVE (from 2026-10-24T00:00Z: not labelled in this phase). A label is PENDING when the read's retrieval time is before `label_matures_at`.
6. Two archives: development reads have boundaries at or before 2026-10-17T00:00Z; holdout reads go to a separate sealed store and archive. Holdout labels are computed by code and written only to a sealed file with its sha256; the phase prints counts and censoring for the holdout, never a class count or prevalence; development statistics are computed by code that never opens the sealed file.
7. The reference pool route CURRENT_VENDOR_LIST uses the first pool of the same vendor pool list (flagged and stratified).
8. Revision sample (P07, PI-D-018), fixed before the first call: the label reads whose sha256 of the request URL ranks in the lowest ceil(10%) are the sample; they are re-read in this session (short gap, measured) and the script for the 48 h re-read is committed with the sample. A re-read at least 48 h after the first read cannot happen before about 2026-10-26T13:00Z; until it runs this item is open, never reported as done.
