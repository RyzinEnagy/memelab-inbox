## Findings

### MATERIAL

M1. Committed tree fails the repository's content guard (A16 is wrong for the committed state). The lead's final suite (06:31Z) ran before the commit (06:38Z), when the SMOKES files were untracked; the guard scans tracked files only. At `c9e894d` the suite is `4 failed, 855 passed`, three of them content-guard tests, because `SMOKES/666ghj__MiroFish/sim_run1/reddit_profiles.json` and `sim_run2/reddit_profiles.json` carry a `bio` key (8 violations; 0 at `77e78ce`). The content is fictional, so no rights problem, but pushing this commit puts a red guard on the branch. Fix: drop or rename the `bio` fields (or store a hash), rerun the full suite after committing, and record the post-commit result.

M2. MAPIE's O1 (5 points) contradicts the lead's own confirmed finding. The award's evidence is "Venn-Abers calibrator output p0p1 (527,2), merged probability", which is the output of the port that MA-1 shows violates the IVAP definition (p1 0.6 instead of 1.0 above the calibration range, reproduced in check 8) and that RANKED_TOP10.md says "must not be used". The VETO table in `check_scorecard.py` vetoes C2 and Q5 for MA-1 but not O1. MAPIE has no other probability output (split conformal returns sets). Under the lead's own rule ("a point that contradicts a confirmed lead finding is refused"), O1 goes to 0: MAPIE 44, rank 11, and lifelines enters the top ten (`p03/skeptic_mutations.py` last line). The lead lists this exact swap as sensitivity reading A, but presents it as a reading choice, not as its veto rule applied consistently. A second inconsistency points the same way: MAPIE earns D1 (3) for passing `sample_weight` through `fit_params` to the wrapped estimator (`classification.py:881`), while crepes, whose `WrapClassifier.fit(X, y, **kwargs)` passes the same keyword to the learner (`base.py:3379`), earns 0, and MAPIE's recommended prefit route never calls `fit`.

M3. purgedcv group k-fold evidence is vacuous, and its k-fold and CPCV splits train on the future. O5 (2 points, "PurgedGroupKFold shared mints 0") and the role "group k-fold by exact mint" in RANKED_TOP10.md and PI-D-028 rest on a split whose training sets are 0, 0, 45, 179 and 397 rows on the challenge (mints spread over the whole month, so each test fold's 24 h windows purge nearly everything). PC-1 reports "leaks 0" for it without fold sizes and states that a zero is not vacuous, which is true only for the KFold control. Separately, PurgedKFold and CPCV, credited as leak-free, train on up to 2,091 and 1,739 rows decided after the test rows; DATA_TIME_CONTRACT requires `label_matures_at <= training-as-of`. This does not move the rank (purgedcv 48 without O5) but it would steer Phases 07 to 12 toward splitters that are empty or future-trained for this data shape. Fix: report train sizes and post-test row counts in PC-1, refuse a group-disjoint award with an empty fold, and restrict the recommended training splitter to walk-forward (k-fold and CPCV only for labelled sensitivity or PBO, never for replay models).

### MINOR

m1. Evidence rule versus checker. PI-D-026 says every point resolves to raw output, a pinned `path:line`, a re-execution or a registry file, but `check_scorecard.py` accepts any `REVIEWS/` path. 247 awarded cells (330 of 1,119 points) cite only review prose and have no lead check (Q2, Q4, Q5, I2 to I4, K1 to K3, L2, L4, L5, C2, C5, R3, R4). Spot checks (sklearn Q2, river and statsmodels K3, interpret K2) found the raw outputs agree, and the load is 11 to 14 points for every top-13 repository, so ranking impact is small.

m2. Checker gaps from check 6 (README or docs at the pin, unrelated existing file, T1 and T3 from prose). Latent; no committed award uses them.

m3. Embargo bound overstated. The conclusion (no leakage on this generator; purge differences are sample-size noise) holds, but "cannot move any metric beyond 0.0001 Brier" is one logistic draw: random row drops move logistic Brier up to 0.00043, and the same purge moves HistGradientBoosting Brier by 0.00086. PHASE_03_RESULTS.json and BLIND_RESCORE_PACKET.md (which tells the rescorer the band "could not move any metric") should say "cannot inflate through label overlap; differences up to about 0.002 are noise".

m4. hazardous C2 (3) is awarded for `recalibrate_survival_function`, an unclipped marginal shift of the any-event survival function rather than a recalibration of the T1 event probability, with evidence citing review prose only (the raw field exists); MAPIE's C2 is vetoed for a definition error while an unclipped shift that can leave [0, 1] passes. hazardous O3 stands despite HZ-2 (cumulative incidence non-monotone for 634 of 634 rows).

m5. Wrapper probabilities treated unevenly: sktime earns O1 (5) for `SklearnClassifierPipeline(LogisticRegression, [SummaryTransformer])`, crepes 0 for passing the learner's probabilities through. With sktime O1 at 0, sktime (41) leaves the top ten, so the claim that eight repositories are stable under every reading depends on this reading.

m6. pandera T4a "fail-closed" depends on how the lab writes the rule. With pandera 0.34.1, a column check `Check.le(decision)` on a `nullable=True` availability column passes a row whose availability time is missing (default `ignore_na=True`), and a negated `gt` check does too (`p03/skeptic_pandera_nan.out.txt`); PA-1 used an explicit `fillna(False)`. Disclosed as reading B; worth a note in the Phase 05 adapter spec.

m7. Data-rights treatment is uneven: mlfinpy is REJECT for UNKNOWN snippet rights, while OASIS stays eligible with bundled Twitter propagation pickles of UNKNOWN rights (no ranking effect; both far from the cut).

m8. TabPFN-2 license paragraph 10 also requires "TabPFN" at the start of the name of any distributed model trained on TabPFN outputs; the gate mentions only the "Built with" display.

m9. Lead totals exceed reviewer totals for 21 of 30 repositories (mean +2.5, top ten +2.9; MAPIE +10, exactly at the "investigate above 10" line). The items were written after the lead read every review and smoke, so "frozen before scoring" does not mean blind to the evidence. Not proof of preference creep, but the blind rescore should look at MAPIE, hazardous (+7) and sktime (+5).

## New counterexample (not P01 to P48 restated)

A splitter certified "zero overlapping training rows" that is useless or future-trained. On the shared challenge, purgedcv's PurgedGroupKFold by exact mint passes every Phase 03 check (0 shared mints, 0 overlapping rows, O5 awarded, `check_scorecard.py` problems 0), yet two of its five folds have no training rows and the others have 45 to 397 of 2,893; PurgedKFold and CPCV also pass with 0 overlaps while training on up to 2,091 rows decided after the test rows. Nothing in the phase catches it: the overlap checkers (R2's `leakcheck.py` and the lead's interval union) test only `[decision, matures)` intersections, PC-1 records no fold sizes, and the scorecard checker never reads evidence content. `p03/skeptic_purgedcv.py` catches it by printing `n_train` and `train_rows_decided_after_last_test` per fold (output `p03/skeptic_purgedcv.out.txt`). A checker rule that refuses a temporal or group award when any fold has fewer than a stated minimum of training rows, or any training row after the earliest test decision for replay-model splitters, would catch it.
