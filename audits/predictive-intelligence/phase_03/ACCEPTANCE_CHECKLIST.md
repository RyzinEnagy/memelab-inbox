# Phase 03 acceptance checklist (written before any deliverable)

Written 2026-10-24T05:46Z by the Phase 03 lead, after the entry gate and after reading the 30 reviewer files, and before any score, rank or deliverable was written. The 30 reviews and the smoke folders were produced earlier by five parallel reviewers under `/home/claude/pi_orchestration/PHASE03_REVIEWER_BRIEF.md`; this phase treats every statement in them as a claim to verify. Each item is falsifiable; PHASE_03_EVIDENCE.md records the command and result for each.

| Id | Check | How it is verified | Fails if |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Package unchanged | `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt` in the package folder | any line not OK |
| A2 | Entry gate | `python -m pytest tests -q` at `77e78ce`, checkout clean apart from the uncommitted `phase_03/` folder | anything other than `1 failed, 858 passed` with only `test_dedup_corroboration_resurface_denial` failing |
| A3 | At least 20 pinned source reviews | `research/REVIEWS/*.md` for all 30 shortlisted rows; every clone that still exists under `/home/claude/pi_research/src/` has HEAD equal to the SHORTLIST.csv pin; the one deleted clone (mlflow) is re-fetched at the pin for the files the lead checks | fewer than 20 reviews, a review of a different SHA, or a clone HEAD that differs from the pin |
| A4 | Reviews verified, not trusted | `research/REVIEW_VERIFICATION.md` and `research/verify/VERIFY_RESULTS.json`: at least one claim per review checked by the lead against the pinned source (file and line) or the raw smoke output, and every claim that drives a REJECT flag or a top-ten place re-checked the same way; a lead re-execution for the claims that only an execution can confirm (purge correctness, causal computation, availability gate, REJECT drivers) | a review with no lead check, a top-ten or REJECT driver resting only on review prose, or a failed check whose score was not corrected |
| A5 | Rubric frozen before scoring | the locked weights of `references/GRADING_RUBRIC.md` operationalized as point items in `research/RUBRIC_ITEMS.csv` (item id, criterion, points, rule, evidence type); item points per criterion sum exactly to the locked weight; file written and hashed before `research/SCORE_ITEMS.csv` exists | an item sum that differs from the locked weight, or items edited after scoring without a dated change note |
| A6 | Uniform scoring with evidence | `research/SCORE_ITEMS.csv` has one row for each of the 30 repositories and each rubric item (30 x items), points either 0 or the item's full value, an evidence reference for every non-zero point (review section plus smoke file, pinned path:line, or PyPI file list), and a reason for zeros where a reviewer had proposed points; unanswered = 0 | a non-zero point without evidence, a partial value inside an item, or a repository without every item |
| A7 | Scorecard recomputes | `research/SCORECARD.csv`: 30 rows, every criterion score equal to the sum of its items, every total equal to the sum of the ten criteria, every criterion within 0 and its locked maximum, reviewers' proposed per-criterion scores and totals beside the lead's | any mismatch (checked by `verify/check_scorecard.py`) |
| A8 | Top ten reproducible | `research/RANKED_TOP10.md` lists exactly the first ten eligible rows of SCORECARD.csv sorted by total descending, ties broken by the written rule; eligibility (no hard-veto REJECT for the recommended route) stated per row; if fewer than ten are eligible the real number is published and the phase is BLOCKED | the published order differs from the CSV sort, or a REJECT row is ranked |
| A9 | Category winners and adoption bundle | `research/CATEGORY_WINNERS.md`: one winner (or an explicit NONE) for each of supervised tabular, time series, online or incremental, calibration and uncertainty, on-chain or anomaly or event time, behavioral multi-agent, evaluation and monitoring, with the evidence that decided it; a best adoption bundle with roles, INTEGRATION_DECISION per role and license obligations | a category missing, or a winner without evidence |
| A10 | License and security gate | `research/LICENSE_AND_SECURITY_GATE.md`: actual root license with sha256 at the pin, per-file or vendored findings, model-weight terms, telemetry and its off switches, install hazards, network exposure, for all 30; P31 and P32 shown caught | a repository missing, or a license stated from a README |
| A11 | Synthetic challenge limitation | the challenge generator, its SHA256SUMS and a note copied to `research/SMOKES/_challenge/`; the 14 h embargo against 24 h labels quantified (val and train overlap counts) and each smoke metric it could affect identified; no rubric point rests on a holdout metric difference that the overlap could inflate | a score that depends on such a metric, or the counts not reproduced |
| A12 | MiroFish | its own section: behavioral scenario generation only, AGPL obligations, LLM and Zep costs, offline mock run results, non-determinism, silent success on LLM failure, P26, P27, P28; no numeric accuracy assigned anywhere | any accuracy number, or a missing item |
| A13 | Blind rescore packet | `research/BLIND_RESCORE_PACKET.md` names the lead's top five plus the sixth and seventh as controls in an order that does not reveal rank, with review and smoke paths and the rubric, and contains no lead score or rank; `research/INDEPENDENT_RESCORE.md` is a placeholder for the orchestrator's blind second reviewer | the packet leaks a lead score, total or order |
| A14 | Positive and negative tests | `verify/check_scorecard.py` passes on the real files; `verify/check_scorecard_negative.py` mutates copies (wrong criterion sum, wrong total, over-maximum, point without evidence, partial item, swapped ranks, REJECT row ranked, README-only evidence for a point, scenario catch removed) and every mutation is refused; P13, P26, P27, P31, P42 each shown caught with a pointer | a mutation accepted, or a scenario without a pointer |
| A15 | Hygiene before commit | scan of `research/SMOKES` and `research/REVIEWS` for secrets and tokens, model weights, tokenizer files, binaries, files over 1 MB and raw third-party social content; anything found excluded or removed and listed | a hit committed |
| A16 | No production code, tracked data untouched | `git diff --name-only 77e78ce..HEAD` lists only `audits/predictive-intelligence/` and `docs/predictive-intelligence/`; full suite after the work shows only the known failure; strays removed by hand; `data/memelab.sqlite` sha256 unchanged | any other path, a new failure, or a stray left |
