## Changes in the repair pass

- Event time: lifelines (44) replaces hazardous (43, was 48) as winner after hazardous lost O3 (non-monotone cumulative incidence, HZ-2) and C2 (recalibration subtracts the absolute error and is unclipped, HZ-3).
- Time series: sktime stays the winner at 41 (was 46): no probability point for a wrapped scikit-learn estimator (ST-4).
- Calibration: MAPIE stays the winner at 44 (was 49): no probability point for its defective Venn-Abers port (MA-1, MA-4).
- Evaluation: purged-cross-validation stays the winner at 48 (was 50); its role is restricted to `WalkForwardSplit` (PC-3).
- Bundle: pandera availability columns must be declared non-nullable (or the check must handle missing values), because a nullable column check passes a row with an unknown availability time (`../skeptic/p03/skeptic_pandera_nan.out.txt`); river keeps no replay-trained model state (RV-4); MAPIE and hazardous exclusions above.
