## 7. Flags and adversarial scenarios

- Flags: none of REJECT, NOT_REALTIME, LEAKAGE_FAIL. The Cox objective is RESEARCH_ONLY until it ships in a release (only the pin build has it).
- P13 and P20: random internal validation inside training would let late rows steer early stopping of a model scored on earlier ones; the wrapper must pass `bags` (time-ordered) or `validation_size=0` with fixed rounds.
- P22: automatic interaction search (`"3x"`) is a model-selection step; record the chosen terms per run.
- P05 and P37: the survival smoke kept censored rows as censored; the lab's PENDING labels must not be encoded as censored at 24 h (they are not yet observed), only VENDOR or UNVERIFIABLE censoring classes could be.
- P31: Apache-2.0 notice for `GaussianDistribution.hpp` if the library is ever redistributed.

## 8. Proposed role and integration decision

- Role: a glassbox alternative to B2 in the Phase 08 model comparison (shape functions are readable in an audit and leaks show up as dominant terms), and a candidate for N05 time-to-barrier research through `survival_cox` once released, with a separate baseline-hazard step needed before any P(event by horizon).
- INTEGRATION_DECISION: INSTALL (released wheel, classification only, pickle files the lab wrote, `bags` for time-ordered validation); survival use ISOLATED_RESEARCH until a release contains it.
- Gap cells addressed: DECISION_RECORDS:MODEL_TRAINING, HIST_MARKET:MODEL_TRAINING (N01); research contribution to N05 (EVENT_LABELS consumers) via survival.
