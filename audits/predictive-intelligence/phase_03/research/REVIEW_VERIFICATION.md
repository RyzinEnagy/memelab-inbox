## Findings about the review records

- **Review times are estimates, not records (MINOR).** In 20 of 30 reviews the stated end time ("Review time (UTC) ... to HH:MM") is later than the file's last write, by up to 31 minutes (feast 06:05Z stated, written 05:33:47Z; statsmodels, TabPFN, interpret, black-it and LightGBM by 15 to 27 minutes). The lead's clock at the entry gate read 05:37Z. Nothing scored uses these times; the reviewer files are left unedited as evidence and this note stands as the correction.
- **Unpurged challenge splits (MINOR).** R1, R3 and R4 fitted or calibrated on rows whose 24 h labels overlap the next split (96 train rows, 45 val rows); R2 purged them. On this generator the overlap cannot inflate a metric (labels independent by construction); differences up to about 0.002 Brier between purged and unpurged fits are sample-size noise (logistic 0.0001, HistGradientBoosting 0.0009, both in the middle of a placebo that drops random rows: `SMOKES/_challenge/NOTE.md`). No point rests on such a metric.
- **A content-guard violation in a smoke input (MATERIAL, skeptic M1).** The MiroFish smoke's two `reddit_profiles.json` files held fictional `bio` strings, which the lab's content guard bars in any tracked JSON file; the first pass ran the suite before committing, so the guard (tracked files only) did not see them. Both strings, and the `persona` strings, are now sha256 and length (`SMOKES/666ghj__MiroFish/PROFILES_TRANSFORMED.txt`).
- **One binary removed.** `SMOKES/lorentzenchr__model-diagnostics/reliability_base_vs_leaky.png` was excluded by the hygiene scan; the review's section 4 still names it; its numbers are in `raw_smoke_model_diagnostics.txt`.
- **Research clone touched.** The lead fetched MiroFish's three tags into its research clone to date them (v0.1.2 at 2026-03-06); HEAD is unchanged (PIN-ALL).

## How the reviewers' proposed scores differed, and why the lead's differ

The reviewers scored on independent scales. The lead applied `RUBRIC_ITEMS.csv` to all 30 with the same evidence rule; reviewer totals stay in SCORECARD.csv. Patterns that explain most differences (lead minus reviewer, totals from -8 to +10):

- **Luck on the fixture earned points from some reviewers.** R1 gave calibration points because raw probabilities fell inside Wilson bins on a logistic fixture (interpret 3, TabPFN 3); the challenge label is a logistic function of the features, so this is not a capability. Lead: 0.
- **Absence of a channel earned temporal points.** R5 gave Mesa and black-it 2 each for having no pretrained knowledge; R3 gave ruptures 3 for a causal refit that is wrapper code. Lead: temporal points only for a library mechanism shown working (no item for absence).
- **Different credit for the same mechanism.** A delayed or censored representation of pending labels (river, lifelines, hazardous, interpret's Cox, NannyML's CBPE, pandera's maturity check, purge exclusion) scored from 0 to 9 temporal points across reviewers; the lead gives each the same 2-point item (T3).
- **Generic hooks scored unevenly.** Sample-weight or cost hooks at the pin (D1, 3 points) were cited by some reviewers and not others; the lead checked each pin (scikit-learn, LightGBM, interpret, statsmodels, lifelines, MAPIE, river, model-diagnostics, NannyML, mlfinpy). In the first pass this was the main reason MAPIE rose by 10 (D1 plus the risk controller in D2); after the repair pass MAPIE is +5, its D1 resting on its own `BinaryRisk` rather than a pass-through `sample_weight`, and its O1 vetoed.
- **Unanswered items.** Where no repeat run had been compared, R1 (determinism) was unanswered; the lead re-ran nine reviewer scripts to answer it uniformly, which moved statsmodels, river, sktime, frouros, pandera, MAPIE, mlfinpy, ruptures and skore by 2 each; the re-run of R1's feast script did the same for feast. `RANKED_TOP10.md` shows the order without these re-runs.
- **Unequal readings of the same mechanism (repair pass, PI-D-029).** Three first-pass awards applied an item more generously to one repository than to another: probabilities of a wrapped third-party estimator (sktime O1 5, crepes 0), a sample weight passed through to the wrapped estimator (MAPIE D1 3, crepes 0) and a point resting on an output a confirmed finding shows defective (MAPIE O1 against MA-1; hazardous O3 against HZ-2; hazardous C2 against HZ-3). One rule each now applies to all 30: a wrapped third-party estimator's probabilities do not count; a pass-through hook does not count (MAPIE keeps D1 through its own `BinaryRisk`); and the verified-finding veto covers every item that credits the defective output.
- **Lead totals above the reviewers' (skeptic m9).** In the first pass the lead's total exceeded the reviewer's for 21 of 30 repositories (mean +2.5; after the repair pass 20 of 30, mean +2.0). The excess is in the near-binary hygiene criteria (integration, code, license, decision and cost: +70, +43, +30, +25 and +19 points summed over the 30), where the items award the full value for a checked fact that reviewers often docked for broader concerns; in temporal integrity, outcomes and calibration the lead awards less than the reviewers (-51, -36, -33). The items were written after reading every review, so they were not blind to the evidence; INDEPENDENT_RESCORE.md and the sensitivity readings are the controls.
